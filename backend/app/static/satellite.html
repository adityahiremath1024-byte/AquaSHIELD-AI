Step-by-Step NDWI Processing Pipeline

STEP 1

Raw Optical Imagery

Ingest PlanetScope 3m commercial optical satellite imagery or ArcGIS World Imagery fallback.

STEP 2

Cloud & Shadow Masking

Exclude bright cloud pixels (brightness > 200) and deep shadows (brightness < 20) to prevent false detections.

STEP 3

NDWI Computation

Compute (Green − NIR) / (Green + NIR) from 8-band analytic data, or (Green − Red) / (Green + Red) from RGB with strict multi-spectral constraints.

STEP 4

Morphological Cleanup

OpenCV opening & closing with 5×5 kernel to remove salt-and-pepper noise and fill small gaps within water bodies.

STEP 5

Connected Component Filter

Label all contiguous water regions and remove every connected component smaller than 80 pixels, eliminating isolated false positives.

STEP 6

Flood Statistics & Risk

Compute surface water %, flooded area sq km, mean NDWI, stagnant water pockets, and flood severity from the cleaned mask.
